This module outlines the purpose and key takeaways from the provided self-study materials on database replication options. The resources cover fundamental concepts of replication, including synchronous and asynchronous replication, leader-based (master-slave) replication, multi-leader replication, and quorum-based replication. Additionally, the module explores real-world use cases, benefits, and trade-offs of different replication approaches. By completing this module, you will gain a deeper understanding of how to achieve fault tolerance, scalability, and data consistency in distributed systems.
