## Overview
This module outlines the purpose and key takeaways from the provided self-study materials on ACID properties and the CAP Theorem. These resources cover fundamental concepts, real-world applications, and deeper insights into the principles of Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, and Durability (ACID) in database systems, as well as the trade-offs between Consistency (C), Availability (A), and Partition Tolerance (P) in distributed systems. Together, these topics provide a comprehensive understanding of the challenges and design considerations in modern data management and distributed architectures.

## Learning Objectives
By reviewing the materials, students will:
- Understand the fundamental principles of the ACID properties.
- Understand the fundamental principles of the CAP theorem.
- Recognize the trade-offs between consistency, availability, and partition tolerance.
- Apply CAP theorem insights to real-world distributed system architectures.
- Explore variations such as eventual consistency and PACELC theorem.
- Gain hands-on knowledge through examples and practical discussions.
