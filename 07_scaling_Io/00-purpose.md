At high concurrency — 1,000 simultaneous clients downloading job history — the bottleneck is rarely CPU or database: it is the volume of bytes moving across the wire. A JSON response for 1,000 records is ~150 KB per request; multiply that by 1,000 concurrent downloaders and you saturate a 1 Gbps NIC before a single database query becomes the problem. The choice of serialization format, compression strategy, and storage layout are therefore first-class architectural decisions, not implementation details.

This module provides an in-depth exploration of Scaling IO, focusing on optimizing data storage, transmission, and processing to improve system performance and scalability. By completing this module, you will gain a comprehensive understanding of how to optimize IO operations across storage, network, and data processing layers — with a concrete focus on choosing and justifying serialization formats under load constraints.
